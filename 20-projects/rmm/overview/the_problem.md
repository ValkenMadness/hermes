What exists today fails Peninsula trail athletes in five specific ways:

1. No existing platform grades Cape Peninsula routes against Peninsula-calibrated terrain standards.
2. No existing platform tells a trail athlete objectively whether they are prepared for a specific route or race.
3. No existing platform scores athlete fitness in a way that accounts for trail terrain, elevation density, and pace consistency simultaneously.
4. No existing platform connects the map, the athlete's performance, and their readiness for specific challenges in a single experience.
5. No existing platform builds gamification on top of GPS-verified, anti-gaming secured performance data.

The gap is not "no one tracks trail running." The gap is "no one understands this specific terrain well enough to grade it honestly, score athletes fairly, and tell them whether they're ready for what they're about to attempt."

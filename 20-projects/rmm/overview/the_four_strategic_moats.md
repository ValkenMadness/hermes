**Moat 1 — Hyper-Local Data.** Every formula is calibrated for Cape Peninsula terrain. The elevation density bands, the TBS thresholds, the benchmark ceilings — all Peninsula-specific. A global platform would need to do this work per geography, which they have no incentive to do.

**Moat 2 — Proprietary Scoring Engine.** Four interconnected formula systems, stress-tested across 8 formula development sessions and validated against real Peninsula GPX data. All formula values are trade secrets under South African common law. The engine cannot be reverse-engineered from outputs alone.

**Moat 3 — GPS-Verified Anti-Gaming.** Ten validation flags on every activity. Leaderboards from official events only. The integrity of the data is the integrity of the brand. No existing platform has this level of data integrity enforcement for a local community.

**Moat 4 — Community Trust.** Peninsula trail athletes are experienced and sceptical. They will stress-test every score and grade. A system that earns their trust creates a moat that money alone cannot breach. This trust is earned locally, on these trails, over time. It cannot be acquired.

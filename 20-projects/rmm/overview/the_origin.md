RMM began as a personal frustration. As a trail runner on the Cape Peninsula, Valken de Villiers found himself navigating a landscape of generic tools — global platforms with no local depth, fitness trackers that counted distance without understanding terrain, grading systems imported from flatter geographies that fundamentally misrepresented Peninsula difficulty.

The Peninsula is not a normal trail environment. A 15 km trail run here involves more elevation, more technical terrain, and more physiological demand than a 15 km trail run in most other parts of the world. The mountains are compact and brutal. A 5 km route can involve 380 metres of elevation gain and scrambling sections that require hands. Generic tools consistently failed to communicate this reality.

RMM was designed and built by a single founder using an AI-assisted development methodology — no external developers, no external funding, full codebase ownership. The platform is live. The engine is complete. The build continues.

- **ValkenMadness Regular:** Brand headlines, logo text, hero expressions only. Never body copy.
- **Supporting fonts:** Everything else. Legibility is primary.
- **Map text halo:** #F5ECD7 at 3px width.
- **Body line height:** Minimum 1.5x.

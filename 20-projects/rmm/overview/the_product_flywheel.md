More athletes on the platform generate more training data. More training data improves benchmark calibration. Better benchmarks produce more accurate scoring. More accurate scoring builds more community trust. More community trust attracts more athletes. More athletes fund more events. More events generate more data. The flywheel accelerates with scale.

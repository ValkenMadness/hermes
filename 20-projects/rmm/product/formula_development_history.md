Complete record of eight formula development sessions, 26–29 March 2026.

**Entry 001 — Research Document V1.** First pass at all systems. Initial RPS weights, 4-grade A–D, initial Race Readiness.

**Entry 002 — V2 Complete System Audit (26 March).** The pivotal session. Complete RPS redesign (weights, decay, consistency modifier, Moving Time, elevation). HR removed from PI. Anti-gaming expanded. 17 decisions locked.

**Entry 003 — Formula Lab Build (27 March).** Full local application built. Three views. Full engine implemented. Validated against 3 Peninsula routes.

**Entry 004 — Formula Lab Enhancement (27 March).** Rename, timestamps, map view, multi-profile, export report, 200 synthetic GPX files.

**Entry 005 — Post-Stress-Test-1 (28 March).** Layer 2 inflation discovered (+19–31%) and removed. Layer 3 redesigned. V's RPS verified correct end-to-end.

**Entry 006 — Stress Test 2 & V1 Lock (28 March).** Three-tier PC system. F10 added. Max Sustained Gradient sign bug fixed. Race Readiness implemented. V1 Engine locked.

**Entry 007 — Route Grading V3 (29 March).** Pure GPS principle established. 6-grade system. 6 ED bands. GPS-derived modifiers. TDS. Validated against 7 real GPX files and 50 modelled routes.

**Entry 008 — RPS Integration / Terrain-Fair (29 March).** Gradient-normalised PC. TDS-adjusted SE. Five new env vars. No weight changes. RPS is now terrain-fair.

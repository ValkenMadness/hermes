### What Exists Today

| Platform | What It Does Well | What It Does NOT Do |
|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| Strava | GPS tracking, social layer, segment leaderboards, massive network effects | No terrain-calibrated grading. No fitness scoring for elevation density. No race readiness. Global generalist. |
| AllTrails | Trail discovery, route maps, reviews, offline maps, 450K+ trails | No performance scoring. No route grading beyond basic difficulty. No event infrastructure. |
| Garmin Connect | Deep biometrics, training load, VO2max, recovery | Device-locked. No independent route grading. No community-calibrated scoring. |
| Komoot | Route planning, turn-by-turn navigation, offline maps | No performance scoring. No athlete assessment. Navigation-first, not intelligence-first. |
| TrainingPeaks | Coach-grade analytics, structured workouts, periodisation | Coaching-focused, not community-focused. No map interface. Professional tool. |
| Wikiloc | User-uploaded GPX trails, global coverage | No scoring, no grading, no performance tracking. Pure route sharing. |

### The Strategic Position

RMM occupies a position that no existing company holds: hyper-local trail intelligence.

|  | Broad (Global) | Deep (Local) |
|--|---------------|-------------|
| Tracking (records activity) | Strava, Garmin Connect | No one |
| Discovery (finds trails) | AllTrails, Komoot, Wikiloc | No one |
| Intelligence (understands terrain, grades routes, scores athletes, assesses readiness) | No one | **RMM** |

### Why Global Platforms Cannot Replicate This Easily

- **Hyper-local calibration:** Every formula is calibrated per geography. Global platforms have no incentive to do this work.
- **Proprietary engine:** Four interconnected systems, stress-tested across 8 sessions, validated against real data. Trade secrets.
- **GPS-verified anti-gaming:** Ten flags per activity. Integrity that money cannot buy.
- **Community trust:** Earned locally, over time. Cannot be acquired.
- **Founder-terrain alignment:** Built by someone who runs these mountains. Not by a product manager looking at a heatmap.

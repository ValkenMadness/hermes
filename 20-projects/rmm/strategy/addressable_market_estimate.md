| Segment | Estimated Size | Basis |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Active trail runners on the Cape Peninsula | 8,000–15,000 | UTCT draws 2,800; multiple trail series draw 500–800 per event; weekly group runs; Strava heatmap density |
| Regular hikers on Table Mountain and surrounds | 30,000–60,000 | Table Mountain sees 800,000+ visitors/year; ~5–10% repeat local hikers |
| Tourists who trail run/hike the Peninsula annually | 20,000–40,000 | 35% of UTCT is international; running tour operators active |
| Road runners who cross-train on Peninsula trails | 5,000–10,000 | Cape Town Marathon: 16K finishers; significant overlap |
| **Total addressable** | **15,000–40,000** | Core trail runners + engaged hikers + visiting athletes |

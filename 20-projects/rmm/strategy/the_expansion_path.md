**Phase 1 (Now):** Cape Peninsula. Prove the model. Build the engine. Earn community trust. Generate first revenue.

**Phase 2 (V2–V3):** Race partnerships. Full platform with Strava/Garmin integration. Leaderboards and social layer. Prove unit economics.

**Phase 3 (V4 — Game Maker):** License the RMM model to operators in other trail geographies. Table Mountain vs Mont Blanc. Each licensed geography gets the same engine calibrated to local terrain. RMM becomes the platform underneath.

Target geographies: Chamonix/Mont Blanc, Swiss Alps, Pyrenees, Scottish Highlands, Drakensberg, Hong Kong, Japan, Vietnam, Patagonia, New Zealand, Pacific Northwest, Colorado.

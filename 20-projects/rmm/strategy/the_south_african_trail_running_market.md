South Africa is a globally significant trail running market:

- RMB Ultra-Trail Cape Town (UTCT): approximately 2,800 runners across six distances. 35% international participation from 55+ countries.
- Comrades Marathon: the world's largest and oldest ultramarathon. Over 10,000 finishers annually since 1988.
- South Africa hosted 129 marathon/ultra events in 2024 (a record), with finisher numbers up 15.2% YoY.
- Trail running participation up 231% over the past decade.
- Academic research (Journal of Sport & Tourism, 2024) identifies South African trail runners as a "lucrative segment of the serious leisure economy."

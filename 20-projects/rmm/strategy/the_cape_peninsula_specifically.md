The Cape Peninsula is not a generic trail area. It is a globally recognised destination:

- Table Mountain National Park: UNESCO World Heritage Site, one of the New7Wonders of Nature.
- 65 named peaks in a compact geography — exceptional elevation density per square kilometre.
- Year-round trail running season (Mediterranean climate).
- Major events: UTCT, Two Oceans (21K and 56K), Cape Town Marathon.
- Established ecosystem: running clubs (CRAG, K-Way VOB, MATES, Celtics, Drifters), weekly group trail runs, specialist stores, running tour operators.

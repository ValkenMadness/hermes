Trail running is one of the fastest-growing segments in the global endurance sports market.

| Source / Metric | 2024 Value | Projected Value | CAGR |
|----------------|-----------|----------------|------|
| Trail Running Market (overall) | USD 4.0B | USD 7.5B by 2032 | 8.0% |
| Trail Running Shoes Market (Grand View) | USD 7.82B (2023) | USD 12.54B by 2030 | 7.1% |
| Trail Running Shoes Market (Straits) | USD 8.44B | USD 14.57B by 2033 | 7.0% |
| Ultra Marathon Market | USD 3.0B | USD 5.5B by 2032 | 9.0% |
| Global Outdoor Recreation Participation (US) | 175.8M Americans (2023) | Growing annually | — |
| Running/Jogging Participants (US, 2024) | 51.05M | Up from 48.31M in 2023 | +5.7% YoY |

Participation growth of 231% over the past decade (RunRepeat data, cited by UTCT Race Director Stuart McConnachie) confirms this is not a fad. The market is structural and accelerating.

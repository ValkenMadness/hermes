### Revenue Stream 1 — Event Entry Fees `[POST-LAUNCH]`

The fastest path to first revenue. GPS-verified timed events with tiered entry:
- Base (R49.95): Participation, leaderboard, digital badge.
- Standard (TBD): Base + physical medal.
- Premium (TBD): Standard + exclusive products.

Three event types: Time Trials, Peak Hunters, Club Face-Offs.

### Revenue Stream 2 — Map Print Sales

Large format illustrated Cape Peninsula trail map. Unit economics: production cost R180-R250, retail R495-R695. Limited pre-sale campaign validates demand and generates float. No platform build required.

### Revenue Stream 3 — Membership Subscription `[POST-LAUNCH]`

R49.95-R79.95/month unlocking: RPS dashboard with full component breakdown, Race Readiness for any route, full performance history with decay visualisation, priority event access, member-only leaderboard visibility. 500 subscribers at R49.95 = R24,975/month. Break-even: ~45 paying members.

### Revenue Stream 4 — Module SaaS Products `[POST-LAUNCH]`

Every formula module is simultaneously a standalone SaaS product. Non-RMM users pay per module ($4.95-$9.95/month). Ops Board already viable standalone. Route Analyzer has global applicability.

### Revenue Stream 5 — Club & Race Partnerships `[POST-LAUNCH]`

Formal partnerships with Peninsula clubs and race organisations. RMM provides event infrastructure, GPS verification, leaderboard system. Partners provide community reach. Revenue share on entry fees.

### Revenue Stream 6 — Game Maker Licensing (V4) `[FUTURE]`

The long-term ceiling. License the RMM model to operators in other mountain running geographies. Licensing fee + revenue share. Cape Town proves the model. RMM becomes the underlying platform for a global network.

### Two-Sided Marketplace `[SCALE]`

Athletes on one side, race organisers on the other. RMM holds proprietary athlete scoring data and a curated audience. Race organisers pay featuring fees. Entry gateway commission (5-10%) on every race processed through RMM.

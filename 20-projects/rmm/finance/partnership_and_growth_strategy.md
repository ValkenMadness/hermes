### Running Stores

Drive foot traffic via leaderboard incentives. Approach after 100+ active athletes and functioning leaderboard.

### Race Organisers

Offer grading system as value-add. Co-branding. Approach after grading system is proven and recognised.

### Tourism Operators

Peninsula trail running as tourism product. Longer-term opportunity requiring larger athlete base.

### Approach

V makes every initial contact personally. Claude drafts partnership proposals from template. Relationships built face-to-face, on trails, at events.

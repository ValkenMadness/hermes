### Route Descriptions `[LAUNCH-CRITICAL]`

Template-based system: V provides field notes + GPS data → Claude drafts from template → V reviews and adjusts voice → publish.

**Template structure:**
```
[Route Name] — [Grade] · [TDS]/10
[Distance] · [Elevation Gain] · [ED] m/km

THE RUN
[2-3 sentences — start, end, terrain, what's distinctive]

WHAT TO EXPECT
[3-4 sentences — hard parts, gradient feel, breaks]

PRACTICAL NOTES
[1-2 sentences — parking, water, escape routes, safety]
```

Launch target: 20–30 core Peninsula routes.

### Social Media `[LAUNCH-CRITICAL]`

**Platforms:** Instagram (primary), Strava Club (community hub).

**Content categories:** Route features (GPS stats make them unique), athlete spotlights, platform updates, Peninsula running culture.

**Frequency:** 3–4 posts per week. Consistency over volume.

**Critical rule:** Do not automate posting at launch. The community is small and tight. They will spot AI content instantly. V's voice and V's photos are the brand. Claude drafts, V finalises and posts.

**Weekly rhythm:** Monday — route feature. Wednesday — training insight or athlete spotlight. Friday — Peninsula culture. Weekend — optional.

**Monthly planning:** Last day of each month, V maps next month's content. 30 minutes. Batch route features with Claude.

### Email Communications `[POST-LAUNCH]`

**Onboarding sequence (automated, 4 emails over 14 days):**
1. Welcome + what RMM does (on signup)
2. First RPS score + component explanation (after first sync)
3. Route recommendations from training data (day 7)
4. Race readiness explanation + target event selection (day 14)

**Ongoing:** Monthly score summary (automated). Event announcements (manual). Platform updates (manual).

POPIA compliance: explicit opt-in, unsubscribe in every email, separate consent for marketing vs transactional.

### Community Management `[LAUNCH-CRITICAL]`

V's job. Not delegatable. When athletes post milestones, V responds personally. When questions arise, V answers honestly. Claude drafts FAQ content and response templates. V personalises.

### Athlete Support `[LAUNCH-CRITICAL]`

**Tier 1 — Self-service.** FAQ page covering RPS, grading, common questions.

**Tier 2 — V responds.** Support email or contact form. Target: 24 hours on weekdays. V can pull data in admin dashboard to explain score changes. Score disputes resolved by transparency.

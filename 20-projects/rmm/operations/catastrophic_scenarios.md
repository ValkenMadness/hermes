**V unavailable 1–2 weeks:** Automated systems continue. Platform serves existing content. Social media and community go quiet. Mitigation: pre-schedule 2 weeks of content. Auto-responder on support email.

**Strava changes API terms/pricing:** Highest-impact external risk. Mitigation: Garmin as secondary from day one. GPX file upload as tertiary. Never depend exclusively on Strava-specific data.

**Competing platform launches on Peninsula:** Formula system, grading methodology, and community relationships are the moat. Focus on deepening moat, not reacting feature-for-feature.
